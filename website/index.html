Two ways to play

When you create a new world, you pick its game mode. Every world keeps its own save.

🗡️

Adventure

Quests, bosses and 13 worlds to conquer.

  • Start with empty hands – mine blocks, craft tools and weapons in 12 tiers
  • Finish three quests per world to wake its boss
  • Beat the boss, find the portal, travel to the next world
  • Tame a flying cow with 5 Aether leaves and ride it through the sky
  • Give your pets names and tell them to sit and wait
🥚

Collector

A world five times bigger, full of creatures.

  • One giant map with 7 biomes: forest, blossoms, desert, jungle, swamp, ice and mushrooms
  • Choose a starter: Sprigbud, Sparkit or Bubbit
  • Turn-based creature battles with 14 types and 42 moves
  • Evolve your creatures from level 15 and level 30
  • Rare shiny creatures – about one in 64
  • Creature Book, team of 6, storage box and 8 craftable balls

60 creatures, 20 families

Every creature is built from blocks and grows when it evolves: a seedling becomes a walking tree, a spark becomes a blazing beast. Pick one and drag it to turn it around.

Craft your own balls

Besides the normal balls from the shop, the Collector mode has eight special balls. Find the materials, open the recipes with Y and craft them at the workbench.

13 worlds

Each Adventure world has its own blocks, monsters, quests and a boss waiting at the end.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse on a computer. On phones and tablets you get touch buttons.
