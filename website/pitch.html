The game

Two game modes in one game

🗡️
Adventure
  • 13 worlds with quests and bosses
  • Craft tools and weapons in 12 tiers
  • Find portals to the next world
  • Tame and ride a flying cow
🥚
Collector
  • A huge world with 7 biomes
